## 2. Strongest Defensible USPs (Evidence-Backed)

### USP 1: Traceable Scientific Auditability (No "Black-Box" AI)
- **The Evidence:** Every single number on the screen can be audited. Clicking any badge opens the original MPPCB annual report at the exact page (e.g. Page 28 of 2025 report). It eliminates hallucination and withstands judicial scrutiny in NGT compliance hearings.

### USP 2: Real-time Multi-Criteria Decision Flexibility
- **The Evidence:** Administrative priorities vary. During heatwaves, DO depletion is paramount; during mass immersion, BOD organic spikes dominate. The live sliders allow BMC engineers to recalibrate weights ($w_{DO}, w_{BOD}, w_{pH}$) and observe instant rank adjustments in 0.1 seconds.

### USP 3: Future-Proof Dynamic Pipeline
- **The Evidence:** The platform is not a static demo. Ingestion of a 500-row 2026/2027 Excel spreadsheet or registration of a new artificial kund instantly recalculates all rankings, updates the GIS map, and generates full trend analytics.
